# User Manual: Logic & Data Interpretation Learning App

## Introduction
Welcome to the Logic & Data Interpretation Learning App! This application is designed to help you build intuition and speed when solving quantitative aptitude problems. 

## Layout Overview
- **Sidebar**: Navigate between the 8 different learning modules, or view your overall Result Summary. Your overall progress (accuracy & questions attempted) is always visible here.
- **Top Header**: The main area displays the title of the module and a short description.
- **Tabs**: Each module is divided into three sections:
  1. **📖 Concept**: Contains detailed theory, formulas, and worked examples to study.
  2. **🧮 Calculator**: Allows you to input your own custom values or datasets. The app will automatically compute the correct answer and show you the exact step-by-step logic used to get there.
  3. **🎯 Practice**: Generates unlimited random questions based on your selected difficulty. Tracks your answer streak and overall module score.

## Modules Explained

### 1. Clock Problems
- Use the **Calculator** to enter any Hour and Minute. It will visually plot the clock face and mathematically walk you through the angle formula: $|30H - 5.5M|$.

### 2. Calendar Problems
- Use the **Calculator** to select any two dates. The app highlights the date on a visual calendar grid and performs a full "Odd Days" breakdown (Centuries + Years + Months + Days) to prove why a date lands on a specific weekday.

### 3. Patterns & Figures
- Explores AP, GP, Fibonacci, Squares, and Alternating series. 
- In **Practice Mode**, always look at the differences between numbers before guessing!

### 4. Tabular Data Interpretation
- The **Calculator** lets you upload your own CSV data, or generate a random financial dataset. It will automatically scan the data for minimums, maximums, averages, and compound growth.

### 5. Bar Graph Analysis
- Switch between **Grouped** and **Stacked** viewing modes to see how visual representation changes data comprehension.

### 6. Pie Chart Analysis
- The **Calculator** accepts a comma-separated list of categories and values, automatically generating a beautifully colored chart and a data table converting values to percentages and degrees.

### 7. Line Graph Analysis
- Turn on the **3-Period Moving Average** toggle in the Calculator to see how smoothing works on spiky data. Automatically detects the period of highest percentage growth.

### 8. Scatter Diagram Analysis
- Analyzes randomly generated Positive, Negative, or Random datasets. Calculates the Pearson Correlation Coefficient ($r$) and draws a regression line ($Y = mX + c$) to predict values.

## Tracking Your Progress
Head to the **Result Summary** page at any time to view:
- A bar chart of your accuracy per module.
- A pie chart of your overall win-rate.
- The **Mistake Analyzer**, which highlights your weakest topic and gives you personalized feedback on your recent errors!
- A button to download a CSV backup of your scores.
